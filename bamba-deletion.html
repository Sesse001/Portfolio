Bamba Account Deletion Request

At Bamba, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal data (POPIA Compliance).

How to delete your account from the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly within the Bamba mobile app:

  1. Open the Bamba app.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Note: This action is immediate and irreversible.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Profile & Auth: Your email, display name, and login credentials are permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication.
  • Learning Progress: All your saved progress, completed tiers, and skipped cards are permanently deleted.
  • Translations/Contributions: To preserve the integrity of the language corpus, your submitted translations are de-identified. Your User ID and personal notes are permanently removed, but the anonymous text remains in the database.

Request deletion via Email

If you are unable to access the app but wish to delete your account, please email us at mabasosenzo190@gmail.com with the subject line "Bamba Account Deletion Request". We will process your request within 7 days.
