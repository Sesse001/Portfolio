Bamba Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 20 November 2026

1. About this policy

Bamba is a South African language-learning and contribution platform. This policy explains what personal information Bamba processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, and how you can ask us to delete it, in accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Bamba, we may process:

  • Account information: Firebase Authentication user ID, email address (if registered), display name, and guest account status.
  • Learning information: Selected language, learning progress, submitted expressions, and review status.
  • Preferences: Reminder preferences (on/off).

Bamba does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos. Bamba does not use advertising IDs or tracking.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and maintain your account, remember your progress, display contributions, operate and protect Bamba, and support language-preservation activities. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Contributions

When you submit a language expression, you are voluntarily contributing to Bamba. Contributions may be displayed to other users and used for language-learning purposes. Do not include private information in contributions.

5. Storage and Security

Bamba uses Google Firebase. Authentication is processed in the US, and Firestore data is stored in South Africa (africa-south1). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.

6. Your Rights and Deletion

You may request deletion of your account at any time via the Profile screen in the app, or by visiting our Account Deletion Page.

7. Children's Privacy

Bamba is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Users aged 13-17 should review this policy with a parent or guardian.
